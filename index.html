Last Night Dossier

Case files, opened nightly

Every mystery is a case file.

Last Night Dossier opens one case at a time: lost ships, missing aviators, creatures nobody has caught and nights the official story never quite explained. The Host walks through the evidence, the theories that hold and the ones that fall apart, and leaves the verdict with you.

Each case comes as a full episode on YouTube, with Shorts, Reels and an X thread that point back to it. Every episode lists its sources. Reconstructions made with AI are labeled as reconstructions.
